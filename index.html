About Horizon Express

Horizon Express Ltd is a licensed public bus transport operator regulated under RURA framework guidelines. Established in 2006, our bus fleet delivers fast travel linking Kigali to the Southern Province.

With modern passenger buses, automated route management, and trained professional drivers, Horizon Express provides safe passenger and courier transport services daily.
